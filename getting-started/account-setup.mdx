After creating your Google Account, a few quick setup steps help protect your data and ensure you can always recover access if something goes wrong. This page covers profile configuration, 2-Step Verification, and account recovery.

## Update your profile

1. Go to [myaccount.google.com](https://myaccount.google.com).
2. Click **Personal info** in the left sidebar.
3. Add or update your:
   - **Profile photo** — helps people identify you in Google Meet and Chat.
   - **Name** — displayed in Gmail, Calendar, and other Google services.
   - **Birthday and gender** — used to personalise your experience.
4. Click **Save** after each change.

## Enable 2-Step Verification

2-Step Verification (2SV) adds a second layer of protection so that even if someone learns your password, they cannot sign in without your phone.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Security settings">
    Go to [myaccount.google.com/security](https://myaccount.google.com/security) and click **2-Step Verification**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your second factor">
    Google supports several options:
    - **Google Prompt** — approve sign-ins on your phone with one tap (recommended).
    - **Authenticator app** — generate time-based codes with Google Authenticator or a compatible app.
    - **SMS / Voice call** — receive a code via text or phone call.
    - **Security key** — use a physical hardware key (strongest option).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Follow the on-screen setup">
    Select your preferred method and complete the guided setup. You will verify a test sign-in before 2SV is activated.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Store backup codes in a safe place. If you lose access to your second factor, backup codes are the fastest way to regain entry to your account.
</Warning>

## Add account recovery options

Recovery options let Google verify your identity when you forget your password or lose access to your account.

| Option | Where to set it | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Recovery email | Personal info → Contact info | Use an email you control outside of Google |
| Recovery phone | Personal info → Contact info | Must be a number you can receive SMS on |
| Security questions | Not available | Google does not use security questions |

## Review connected apps

Third-party apps you have authorised can access parts of your Google Account. To review and remove access:

1. Go to [myaccount.google.com/permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).
2. Click any app to see what data it can access.
3. Click **Remove access** to revoke permissions for apps you no longer use.

<Tip>
  Audit connected apps periodically — removing unused apps reduces your account's attack surface.
</Tip>

## Language and region

To change your display language or region:

1. Go to [myaccount.google.com](https://myaccount.google.com) → **Data & privacy** → **General preferences for the web**.
2. Click **Language** or **Search settings** to adjust your preferences.
3. Changes take effect across Google services within a few minutes.
