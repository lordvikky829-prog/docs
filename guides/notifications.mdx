Google sends notifications across email, browser push alerts, and mobile apps. This guide explains how to manage notification settings for each major service so you stay informed without being overwhelmed.

## Gmail notifications

Gmail can notify you about new emails via desktop browser notifications or the mobile app.

**Desktop browser notifications:**
1. Open [mail.google.com](https://mail.google.com).
2. Click the **Settings gear** (top-right) → **See all settings**.
3. On the **General** tab, find **Desktop notifications**.
4. Choose one of:
   - **New mail notifications on** — notify for all incoming mail.
   - **Important mail notifications on** — notify only for mail Gmail marks as important.
   - **Mail notifications off** — disable desktop notifications.
5. Click **Save Changes** at the bottom.

**Mobile push notifications:**
Open the Gmail app → tap the three-bar menu → **Settings** → tap your account → **Notifications** to control notification frequency and label-specific alerts.

## Google Drive notifications

Drive notifies you when someone shares a file with you, comments on your file, or requests access.

To manage Drive notifications:
1. Open [drive.google.com](https://drive.google.com).
2. Click the **Settings gear** → **Settings**.
3. Under **Notifications**, toggle individual notification types on or off.

For file-level notifications on a specific Google Doc, Sheet, or Slide:
1. Open the file.
2. Click the **comment bell icon** (top-right).
3. Choose **All comments**, **Only yours**, or **None**.

## Google Calendar reminders

1. Open [calendar.google.com](https://calendar.google.com).
2. Click the **Settings gear** → **Settings** → **Event settings**.
3. Set default notification timing (e.g., 10 minutes before every event).

For individual events:
1. Click the event.
2. Click the pencil icon to edit.
3. Under **Notifications**, click **Add notification** and choose email or desktop, plus timing.

## Google Meet notifications

Meet sends email notifications for scheduled meetings. To control them:

1. Notifications for meetings come through Google Calendar — manage them in Calendar settings.
2. For Meet-specific settings, open the Google Meet app on mobile → **Settings → Notifications**.

## Mobile app notification settings

For any Google app on Android or iOS, you can control notifications at the system level:

- **Android:** Settings → Apps → [App name] → Notifications.
- **iOS:** Settings → Notifications → [App name].

System-level settings override in-app settings for push notifications.

<Tip>
  Use Gmail's notification setting for **Important mail only** to reduce noise — Gmail's priority algorithm filters out newsletters and automated messages.
</Tip>
