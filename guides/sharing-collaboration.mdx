Google Drive and Workspace make it easy to share files and collaborate with colleagues, clients, or the public. This guide explains how to control access, collaborate in documents, and manage shared content.

## Share a file or folder

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the share dialog">
    Right-click the file or folder in Drive and select **Share**, or open the file and click the **Share** button in the top-right corner.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add people or groups">
    Type email addresses in the **Add people and groups** field. You can add individuals, Google Groups, or distribution lists. Choose a permission level for each person:

    | Permission | What they can do |
    |---|---|
    | Viewer | Read the file, no changes |
    | Commenter | Add and reply to comments |
    | Editor | Make changes, add comments, re-share |
  </Step>

  <Step title="Send a notification">
    Add an optional message in the notification field and click **Send**. Recipients get an email with a direct link to the file.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Share with a link

To share with anyone who has the URL:

1. Open the Share dialog.
2. Under **General access**, click the dropdown and choose **Anyone with the link**.
3. Set the link permission to Viewer, Commenter, or Editor.
4. Click **Copy link** and share the URL.

<Warning>
  "Anyone with the link" means anyone who obtains the URL can access the file. Do not use this for sensitive information unless you intend it to be public.
</Warning>

## Collaborate with comments and suggestions

**Add a comment:**
1. Select text or click a location in a Google Doc, Sheet, or Slide.
2. Press **Ctrl+Alt+M** (Windows) or **Cmd+Option+M** (Mac), or click the comment icon in the toolbar.
3. Type your comment and click **Comment**.
4. Tag a person with `@email` to send them a notification.

**Suggest edits (Docs only):**
1. In a Google Doc, go to the pencil icon (top-right of the editing area) and select **Suggesting**.
2. Make your edits — they appear as tracked changes in a distinct colour.
3. Document owners and editors can accept or reject each suggestion.

## Manage existing shares

To see who has access and change permissions:

1. Open the Share dialog.
2. Scroll down to **People with access**.
3. Click the permission level next to any person to change it or remove their access.

## Transfer ownership

If you need to hand a file to another person permanently:

1. Open the Share dialog.
2. Next to the person's name, click their permission level and select **Transfer ownership**.
3. The new owner receives a confirmation email.

<Tip>
  Use Shared drives for team content so ownership isn't tied to a single person. Files in Shared drives belong to the team, not the individual.
</Tip>
