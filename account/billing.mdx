Google One is the subscription service that adds extra storage across Google Drive, Gmail, and Google Photos beyond the free 15 GB. This page explains how to manage your plan, update billing details, and view payment history.

## View your current plan

1. Go to [one.google.com](https://one.google.com).
2. Your current storage plan and usage are displayed on the home screen.
3. Click **Manage** to see plan details, renewal date, and billing frequency.

## Upgrade or change your plan

1. Open [one.google.com/storage](https://one.google.com/storage).
2. Browse available plans:

| Plan | Storage | Monthly price |
|---|---|---|
| Free | 15 GB | $0 |
| Basic | 100 GB | $1.99 |
| Standard | 200 GB | $2.99 |
| Premium | 2 TB | $9.99 |
| Premium | 5 TB | $24.99 |

3. Click **Get** next to the plan you want.
4. Confirm payment and click **Subscribe**.

You are charged immediately and storage is upgraded right away. Annual billing options are available at a discounted rate.

## Update your payment method

1. Go to [one.google.com](https://one.google.com) → **Settings → Manage subscription → Payment methods**.
2. Click **Add a payment method** to add a new credit or debit card.
3. To remove an existing card, click the three-dot menu next to it and select **Remove**.

<Note>
  Payment methods for Google subscriptions are managed through Google Pay. Visit [pay.google.com](https://pay.google.com) to see all your saved payment methods.
</Note>

## View billing history

1. Go to [pay.google.com](https://pay.google.com).
2. Click **Activity** in the left sidebar.
3. Click any transaction to see a receipt with the date, amount, and subscription name.

## Cancel your subscription

1. Open [one.google.com](https://one.google.com) → **Settings → Manage subscription**.
2. Click **Cancel subscription** and follow the confirmation steps.
3. Your plan stays active until the end of the current billing period, after which storage reverts to the free 15 GB.

<Warning>
  If your total storage usage exceeds 15 GB after cancellation, you will not lose any data immediately — but you will not be able to upload new files, send emails, or add to Google Photos until you free up space or resubscribe.
</Warning>

## Google One benefits

In addition to extra storage, Google One plans include:

- **VPN by Google One** (on Standard plans and above) — encrypted VPN for your phone and computer.
- **Google Store discount** — 10% back on Google Store purchases (Premium plan).
- **Google expert support** — live chat or call with a Google specialist.
- **Family sharing** — share your storage plan with up to 5 family members.
