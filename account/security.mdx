Your Google Account security settings give you control over how you sign in, which devices have access, and which third-party apps can use your data. This page covers everything you can do to keep your account safe.

## Security Checkup

Google's Security Checkup analyses your account and recommends actions to improve security.

1. Go to [myaccount.google.com/security-checkup](https://myaccount.google.com/security-checkup).
2. Review each section — Recent security activity, Your devices, Saved passwords, 2-Step Verification, and Third-party access.
3. Follow the recommended actions for any warnings flagged in red or yellow.

## 2-Step Verification

2-Step Verification (2SV) requires a second form of confirmation in addition to your password when signing in.

### Enable 2-Step Verification

1. Go to [myaccount.google.com/signinoptions/two-step-verification](https://myaccount.google.com/signinoptions/two-step-verification).
2. Click **Get started** and follow the prompts.
3. Choose your second factor:

| Method | Security level | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Google Prompt | High | Tap a prompt on your phone |
| Authenticator app | High | Time-based codes from an app |
| Passkeys | Highest | Biometric or PIN on your device |
| SMS / Voice | Medium | Code sent via text or call |
| Backup codes | Medium | Pre-generated one-time codes |

### Passkeys

Passkeys replace passwords with biometric or PIN authentication stored on your device.

1. Go to [myaccount.google.com/passkeys](https://myaccount.google.com/passkeys).
2. Click **Use passkeys**.
3. Follow the prompt to create a passkey using Face ID, fingerprint, or your device PIN.

## Manage devices

To see which devices are signed in to your Google Account:

1. Go to [myaccount.google.com/device-activity](https://myaccount.google.com/device-activity).
2. Review the list of devices.
3. Click a device you do not recognise and select **Sign out** to revoke access.

## Password security

**Change your password:**
1. Go to [myaccount.google.com/signinoptions/password](https://myaccount.google.com/signinoptions/password).
2. Enter your current password, then your new password twice.
3. Click **Change password**.

**Password Manager:**
Google Password Manager saves and auto-fills passwords across Chrome and Android. Access your saved passwords at [passwords.google.com](https://passwords.google.com).

## Recent security activity

Google logs significant account events such as sign-ins from new devices and password changes.

1. Go to [myaccount.google.com/notifications](https://myaccount.google.com/notifications) to review recent activity.
2. If you see an event you do not recognise, click **No, secure account** immediately and follow the recovery steps.

<Warning>
  If you see suspicious activity, act immediately — change your password, review connected apps, and enable 2-Step Verification if you have not already.
</Warning>
