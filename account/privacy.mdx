Google gives you tools to control your data and privacy across all its services. This page explains how to use Privacy Checkup, manage activity controls, adjust ad personalisation, and download or delete your data.

## Privacy Checkup

Privacy Checkup walks you through the most important privacy settings in one guided flow.

1. Go to [myaccount.google.com/privacycheckup](https://myaccount.google.com/privacycheckup).
2. Step through each section: Activity controls, Ad personalisation, YouTube settings, and more.
3. Adjust settings and click **Confirm** on each step.

## Activity controls

Activity controls determine whether Google saves your activity data to personalise your experience.

| Control | What it tracks | Where to manage |
|---|---|---|
| Web & App Activity | Searches, sites visited, app activity | [myactivity.google.com/activitycontrols](https://myactivity.google.com/activitycontrols) |
| Location History | Places you visit | [myactivity.google.com/activitycontrols](https://myactivity.google.com/activitycontrols) |
| YouTube History | Videos watched and searched | [myactivity.google.com/activitycontrols](https://myactivity.google.com/activitycontrols) |
| Ad personalisation | Interests used for ad targeting | [adssettings.google.com](https://adssettings.google.com) |

To pause any control:
1. Click the control name.
2. Toggle it off and click **Pause**.

## Delete your activity

You can delete activity data manually or set auto-deletion:

**Manual deletion:**
1. Go to [myactivity.google.com](https://myactivity.google.com).
2. Click **Delete activity by** → choose a time range or service → **Delete**.

**Auto-delete:**
1. Go to [myactivity.google.com/activitycontrols](https://myactivity.google.com/activitycontrols).
2. Click a control (e.g., Web & App Activity) → **Auto-delete activity older than**.
3. Choose 3 months, 18 months, or 36 months.

## Ad settings

Google uses your activity to personalise the ads you see. To control this:

1. Go to [adssettings.google.com](https://adssettings.google.com).
2. Review the interest categories Google has inferred about you.
3. Turn off any category you do not want used for ad targeting, or toggle **Ad personalisation** off entirely.

<Note>
  Turning off ad personalisation does not reduce the number of ads you see — it means the ads will be less relevant to your interests.
</Note>

## Download your data (Google Takeout)

Google Takeout lets you export a copy of all your data.

1. Go to [takeout.google.com](https://takeout.google.com).
2. Select the Google services whose data you want to export.
3. Choose an export format (ZIP, TGZ) and delivery method (email, Drive, Dropbox, or OneDrive).
4. Click **Create export** — Google emails you when the archive is ready to download.
