Your Google Account profile controls how your name, photo, and contact details appear across Google services like Gmail, Meet, and Chat. This page explains how to view and update every part of your profile.

## View your profile

1. Go to [myaccount.google.com](https://myaccount.google.com).
2. Click **Personal info** in the left sidebar.
3. You will see sections for Basic info, Contact info, and Work info.

## Update your name

1. In **Personal info**, click **Name**.
2. Edit your first and last name.
3. Click **Save**.

<Note>
  Your name is visible to people you email, share files with, and video call. Use a name you are comfortable sharing in a professional context.
</Note>

## Change your profile photo

Your profile photo appears in Gmail, Calendar, Meet, Drive, and other Google services.

1. In **Personal info**, click your current photo or the photo placeholder.
2. Click **Select a photo from your device** or choose an existing photo from your Google Photos library.
3. Crop the photo using the on-screen tool.
4. Click **Set as profile photo**.

## Update contact information

Contact info includes your recovery email and recovery phone number, which Google uses for account verification and recovery.

1. In **Personal info**, click **Contact info**.
2. Update your recovery email or phone number.
3. Google sends a verification message — complete the verification to save the change.

<Warning>
  Keep recovery information current. Outdated recovery details can prevent you from regaining account access if you forget your password.
</Warning>

## Set your birthday

Some Google features use your birthday to provide age-appropriate content and services.

1. In **Personal info**, click **Birthday**.
2. Enter your birth date.
3. Use the **Who can see this** dropdown to choose your preferred visibility: Only you, or anyone who can see your public profile.

## Language and region preferences

To change the language used across Google services:

1. Go to [myaccount.google.com](https://myaccount.google.com).
2. Click **Data & privacy → General preferences for the web**.
3. Click **Language** and choose your preferred language.
4. Click **Select** to apply.

For search language and region specifically, visit [google.com/preferences](https://www.google.com/preferences).

## Delete your account

Deleting your Google Account permanently removes all data including emails, Drive files, and photos.

1. Go to [myaccount.google.com](https://myaccount.google.com) → **Data & privacy → Delete your Google Account**.
2. Review the list of data that will be deleted.
3. Check the confirmation boxes and click **Delete account**.

<Warning>
  Account deletion is irreversible. Download your data first using [Google Takeout](https://takeout.google.com) before deleting.
</Warning>
