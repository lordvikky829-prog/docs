## Get started in three steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your Google Account">
    Visit [accounts.google.com](https://accounts.google.com) and sign up with your email address. Your account gives you access to all Google services.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Explore your dashboard">
    Open [myaccount.google.com](https://myaccount.google.com) to manage your profile, security settings, and connected apps.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start using services">
    Jump straight into [Search](https://google.com), open [Drive](https://drive.google.com), or compose your first email in [Gmail](https://mail.google.com).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  All Google services share the same account — sign in once and everything is connected.
</Tip>
