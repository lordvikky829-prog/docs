This guide walks you through creating your Google Account and accessing the most popular services for the first time. By the end you will be able to search the web, upload a file to Drive, and send an email.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a Google Account">
    1. Go to [accounts.google.com/signup](https://accounts.google.com/signup).
    2. Enter your first name, last name, and choose a Gmail address (or use your existing email).
    3. Set a strong password — at least 8 characters with a mix of letters, numbers, and symbols.
    4. Follow the on-screen prompts to verify your phone number and agree to Google's Terms of Service.

    <Tip>
      Choose a Gmail address that represents you professionally — you will use it across all Google services.
    </Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Sign in to your account">
    After creating your account, you are signed in automatically. On future visits, go to [google.com](https://google.com) and click **Sign in** in the top-right corner. Enter your email and password to access all services.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Search the web">
    Type any query into the search bar at [google.com](https://google.com) and press **Enter** or click the search icon. Use the tabs at the top of results — **All**, **Images**, **Videos**, **Maps**, **News** — to filter by content type.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Upload a file to Google Drive">
    1. Open [drive.google.com](https://drive.google.com).
    2. Click **+ New** and select **File upload**.
    3. Choose a file from your device — it uploads immediately.
    4. Click the file name to open and preview it in your browser.

    Every Google Account includes 15 GB of free storage shared across Drive, Gmail, and Google Photos.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Send your first email">
    1. Open [mail.google.com](https://mail.google.com).
    2. Click **Compose** (bottom-left).
    3. Enter a recipient address, subject line, and message body.
    4. Click **Send**.
  </Step>
</Steps>
