Google Drive is your cloud storage hub. Upload any file type, organise content into folders, and access everything from a browser or the Drive desktop app. Storage is shared across Drive, Gmail, and Google Photos, with 15 GB included free on every Google Account.

## Upload files and folders

**From the browser:**
1. Open [drive.google.com](https://drive.google.com).
2. Click **+ New** and select **File upload** or **Folder upload**.
3. Select files from your device — they appear in Drive as soon as the upload completes.

**Drag and drop:**
Drag files directly from your desktop into the Drive browser window to upload them.

**Desktop app:**
Install [Google Drive for desktop](https://www.google.com/drive/download/) to sync a folder on your computer with Drive automatically.

## Organise with folders

- Click **+ New → Folder** to create a folder.
- Drag files into folders to organise them.
- Right-click any file or folder for options: rename, move, add to starred, share, and more.
- Use **Starred** (left sidebar) to bookmark frequently accessed items.

## Search your Drive

Use the search bar at the top of Drive to find files by name, type, or content:

| Search tip | Example |
|---|---|
| Search by file type | `type:spreadsheet budget` |
| Search by owner | `owner:colleague@example.com` |
| Search by date modified | `before:2024-01-01` |
| Search inside documents | Type any word that appears in the file |

## Share files and folders

1. Right-click a file or folder and select **Share**.
2. Enter one or more email addresses.
3. Set the permission level:
   - **Viewer** — can only view, not edit or comment.
   - **Commenter** — can add comments but not edit.
   - **Editor** — can make changes and share with others.
4. Optionally, click **Copy link** to share with anyone who has the link.

<Warning>
  Files shared with "Anyone with the link" are accessible to anyone who obtains the URL. Use this setting only for public content.
</Warning>

## Google Drive desktop sync

Install Google Drive for desktop to access Drive files without opening a browser:

1. Download from [google.com/drive/download](https://www.google.com/drive/download/).
2. Sign in with your Google Account.
3. Choose folders on your computer to sync to Drive, and choose Drive folders to make available offline.
