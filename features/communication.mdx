Google's communication tools — Gmail, Google Meet, and Google Chat — let you stay connected whether you are writing an email, joining a video meeting, or sending a quick message. All three are tied to your Google Account and work together seamlessly.

## Gmail

Gmail is Google's email service, available at [mail.google.com](https://mail.google.com).

### Compose and send email

1. Click **Compose** (bottom-left).
2. Enter recipient addresses in **To**, **Cc**, or **Bcc**.
3. Write your subject line and message body.
4. Attach files by clicking the paperclip icon, or drag files into the compose window.
5. Click **Send**.

### Organise your inbox

- **Labels** — apply colour-coded labels to categorise messages (similar to folders, but a single email can have multiple labels).
- **Filters** — automatically label, archive, delete, or forward incoming messages based on rules (Settings → Filters and Blocked Addresses → Create a new filter).
- **Starred messages** — click the star icon to flag important emails for easy retrieval.
- **Snooze** — hover over an email and click the clock icon to hide it until a later time.

### Search email

Use the Gmail search bar with operators to find specific messages:

| Operator | Example | Finds |
|---|---|---|
| `from:` | `from:boss@example.com` | Emails from a specific sender |
| `to:` | `to:me` | Emails sent to you |
| `subject:` | `subject:invoice` | Emails with a keyword in the subject |
| `has:attachment` | `has:attachment` | Emails with attachments |
| `is:unread` | `is:unread` | Unread messages |

## Google Meet

Google Meet provides high-quality video and audio calls for individuals and groups.

### Start or join a meeting

**Start a new meeting:**
1. Go to [meet.google.com](https://meet.google.com).
2. Click **New meeting** and choose **Start an instant meeting** or **Create a meeting for later**.
3. Share the meeting link with participants.

**Join a meeting:**
1. Click the link shared by the organiser, or go to [meet.google.com](https://meet.google.com) and enter the meeting code.
2. Preview your camera and microphone, then click **Join now**.

### During a call

- **Mute / unmute** — press the microphone icon or use the keyboard shortcut **Ctrl+D** (Windows) / **Cmd+D** (Mac).
- **Turn camera on / off** — press the camera icon or **Ctrl+E** / **Cmd+E**.
- **Present your screen** — click the **Present now** button in the bottom bar and choose a window or your entire screen.
- **Raise hand** — let the host know you want to speak without interrupting.
- **Captions** — enable live captions by clicking the **CC** button.

## Google Chat

Google Chat is a messaging app for direct messages and group spaces.

### Direct messages

1. Open [chat.google.com](https://chat.google.com).
2. Click **New chat** and enter a contact's name or email.
3. Type your message and press **Enter** to send.

### Spaces

Spaces are persistent group conversations tied to a project or topic.

1. Click **+** next to **Spaces** and select **Create a space**.
2. Name the space, add members, and optionally link a Google Drive folder.
3. Use threads inside spaces to keep related messages organised.

<Tip>
  Pin important messages in a Space by hovering over the message and clicking the three-dot menu → **Pin message**.
</Tip>
