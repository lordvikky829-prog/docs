Google Search helps you find information across the web and your personal content. Beyond basic queries, you can use search operators, voice search, and specialised search tools to narrow results and find exactly what you need.

## Basic search

Type your query into the search bar at [google.com](https://google.com) and press **Enter**. Google returns the most relevant results based on your words, your location, and your search history.

### Result types

Use the tabs at the top of any search results page to filter by content type:

| Tab | What it shows |
|---|---|
| All | Blended results across all types |
| Images | Photos, illustrations, and screenshots |
| Videos | YouTube and web videos |
| News | Articles from news publishers |
| Maps | Local businesses and places |
| Shopping | Products from online retailers |
| Books | Book previews and information |

## Search operators

Refine your queries with these operators:

| Operator | Example | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `"quotes"` | `"climate change"` | Search for an exact phrase |
| `-` | `jaguar -car` | Exclude a word from results |
| `site:` | `site:bbc.co.uk` | Search within a specific site |
| `filetype:` | `filetype:pdf annual report` | Find a specific file type |
| `OR` | `cat OR dog` | Return results matching either term |
| `intitle:` | `intitle:python tutorial` | Results where title contains the word |
| `before:` / `after:` | `news after:2024-01-01` | Filter by date |

## Safe Search

Safe Search filters explicit content from results. To change your Safe Search setting:

1. Go to [google.com/safesearch](https://google.com/safesearch).
2. Choose **Filter**, **Blur**, or **Off**.
3. Click **Save**.

<Note>
  On school or work networks, administrators may lock Safe Search and prevent changes.
</Note>

## Voice search

To search by voice on desktop:

1. Go to [google.com](https://google.com).
2. Click the **microphone icon** inside the search bar.
3. Allow microphone access if prompted.
4. Speak your query clearly.

On mobile, tap the microphone icon in the Google app or in your browser's address bar.

## Google Lens

Google Lens lets you search using an image instead of text.

1. Click the **camera icon** in the search bar.
2. Upload an image or paste a URL.
3. Google identifies objects, text, places, or products in the image and returns relevant results.

## Search history

Google saves your search history when you are signed in to personalise future results. To view or delete your history:

1. Go to [myactivity.google.com](https://myactivity.google.com).
2. Use filters to browse or search your activity.
3. Click the three-dot menu next to any item and select **Delete** to remove it, or use **Delete activity by** to bulk-delete by date range or service.

<Tip>
  Use Incognito mode (Ctrl+Shift+N / Cmd+Shift+N) to search without saving activity to your account.
</Tip>
