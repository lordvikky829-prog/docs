This page answers the questions users ask most often about Google services. If you do not find your answer here, visit the [common issues guide](/support/common-issues) or [contact support](/support/contact).

## Account

<Accordion title="How do I create a Google Account?">
  Go to [accounts.google.com/signup](https://accounts.google.com/signup). Enter your name, choose a Gmail address or use your existing email, set a password, and complete the verification steps. Your account gives you access to all Google services immediately.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="I forgot my password. How do I reset it?">
  Go to [accounts.google.com/signin/recovery](https://accounts.google.com/signin/recovery). Enter your email address, then follow the identity verification steps — Google will send a recovery code to your recovery phone number or recovery email address.

  If you have not set recovery options, Google may ask security questions about your account activity to verify your identity.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I change my Gmail address?">
  You cannot change an existing Gmail address directly. However, you can:
  - Create a new Google Account with a different Gmail address.
  - Set up email forwarding from your old account to your new one (Gmail Settings → See all settings → Forwarding and POP/IMAP → Add a forwarding address).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do I sign out of Google on all devices?">
  Go to [myaccount.google.com/device-activity](https://myaccount.google.com/device-activity). You will see a list of all devices currently signed in to your account. Click **Sign out** next to any device you want to remove, or use **Sign out of all devices** to sign out everywhere at once.
</Accordion>

## Storage

<Accordion title="How much free storage do I get?">
  Every Google Account includes **15 GB of free storage**, shared across Google Drive, Gmail, and Google Photos. To check your current usage, go to [one.google.com/storage](https://one.google.com/storage).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="My Drive storage is full. What can I do?">
  You have two options:
  1. **Free up space** — delete large files, email attachments, or photos you no longer need. Use [one.google.com/storage/management](https://one.google.com/storage/management) to see the largest files in your account.
  2. **Upgrade storage** — subscribe to a Google One plan at [one.google.com/storage](https://one.google.com/storage) for 100 GB, 200 GB, 2 TB, or more.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Do Google Docs, Sheets, and Slides count against my storage?">
  No. Files created in Google Docs, Sheets, Slides, Forms, and Sites do **not** count against your 15 GB storage quota. Only files you upload (PDFs, photos, videos, etc.) and emails with attachments count.
</Accordion>

## Gmail

<Accordion title="Why is email going to Spam?">
  Gmail's spam filter occasionally misclassifies legitimate email. To prevent this:
  - Open the email in Spam and click **Not Spam** to train the filter.
  - Add the sender's address to your contacts.
  - Create a filter (Settings → Filters and Blocked Addresses → Create a new filter) to always deliver email from that address to your inbox.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do I unsubscribe from mailing lists in Gmail?">
  Open the email, click the three-dot menu at the top-right of the message, and select **Unsubscribe**. Gmail sends an unsubscribe request to the sender and moves future emails from that list to spam.
</Accordion>

## Billing

<Accordion title="How do I cancel my Google One subscription?">
  Go to [one.google.com](https://one.google.com) → Settings → Manage subscription → **Cancel subscription**. Your plan remains active until the end of the current billing period, after which storage reverts to the free 15 GB tier.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Where can I find my Google payment receipts?">
  All Google payment receipts are in Google Pay. Go to [pay.google.com](https://pay.google.com) and click **Activity** to see a full transaction history with receipts.
</Accordion>
