Google offers several support channels depending on your account type and the service you need help with. This page helps you find the fastest path to a resolution.

## Google Help Center

The fastest way to resolve most issues is to search the Help Center:

1. Go to [support.google.com](https://support.google.com).
2. Click the service you need help with (Gmail, Drive, Meet, etc.).
3. Browse articles or type your issue in the search bar.

Most questions about account access, billing, and service features are covered by detailed articles.

## Google One expert support

If you have a Google One subscription (Basic 100 GB or above), you have access to Google expert support via live chat or phone.

1. Open the Google One app or go to [one.google.com](https://one.google.com).
2. Tap **Support** in the bottom navigation.
3. Choose **Chat with an expert** or **Call an expert**.
4. Describe your issue — an agent will assist you in real time.

## Report abuse or safety issues

- **Report a phishing email:** In Gmail, open the email, click the three-dot menu, and select **Report phishing**.
- **Report spam:** Click **Report spam** in Gmail's toolbar.
- **Report a safety concern or policy violation:** Go to [google.com/contact](https://www.google.com/contact) and select the relevant category.
