This guide covers the most common issues users encounter with Google services and provides clear steps to resolve each one.

## Sign-in problems

### Cannot sign in — wrong password

1. Go to [accounts.google.com/signin/recovery](https://accounts.google.com/signin/recovery).
2. Enter your email address and click **Next**.
3. Click **Forgot password?** and follow the verification steps to reset your password.

### Account locked after too many failed attempts

Google temporarily locks sign-in after multiple failed attempts to protect your account. Wait at least 1 hour and try again. If the problem persists, use the account recovery flow at [accounts.google.com/signin/recovery](https://accounts.google.com/signin/recovery).

### Two-factor verification code not arriving

- **SMS code not arriving:** Check that your phone has signal and that the number on file is correct. Request a new code — old codes expire after a few minutes.
- **Authenticator app code rejected:** The code may have expired. Wait for the app to generate a new 6-digit code and try again. Ensure your phone's time is synchronised correctly (device Settings → General → Date & Time → Set Automatically).
- **Lost access to 2FA device:** Use a backup code from the list you saved when setting up 2SV. Go to [myaccount.google.com/signinoptions/two-step-verification](https://myaccount.google.com/signinoptions/two-step-verification) once signed in to update your 2FA method.

## Google Drive issues

### Files not syncing with Drive for Desktop

1. Check that Drive for Desktop is running — look for the Drive icon in your system tray or menu bar.
2. Click the Drive icon and check the sync status. If it shows an error, click the error message for details.
3. Sign out of Drive for Desktop and sign back in.
4. Restart the Drive for Desktop application.
5. If sync still fails, check your internet connection and firewall settings — Drive requires outbound access on port 443.

### Cannot upload files to Drive

- **Storage full:** Check your storage usage at [one.google.com/storage](https://one.google.com/storage). Delete files or upgrade your plan.
- **File type blocked:** Some file types are blocked for security reasons. Convert the file to a different format if needed.
- **File too large:** The maximum single file upload size is 5 TB. Files over this limit must be split.

## Gmail delivery issues

### Emails not arriving

1. Check your **Spam** folder — the email may have been filtered incorrectly.
2. Check active filters in Settings → **Filters and Blocked Addresses** — a filter may be archiving or deleting the email.
3. Confirm the sender has the correct email address.
4. If you use email forwarding, verify the forwarding address is still active.

### Cannot send emails

- **Daily send limit reached:** Gmail limits free accounts to 500 emails per day. Workspace accounts have higher limits. Wait 24 hours for the limit to reset.
- **Recipient address bounced:** The recipient's email address may be invalid. Verify the address and try again.

## Google Meet issues

### Poor video or audio quality

1. Check your internet connection — Meet performs best with at least 3 Mbps download and upload speed.
2. Turn off your camera to reduce bandwidth usage if audio quality is poor.
3. Close other applications or browser tabs that are using the internet.
4. Switch to a wired connection instead of Wi-Fi if possible.
5. In Meet, click the three-dot menu → **Settings → Video** and reduce the send and receive resolution to **360p**.

### Camera or microphone not working in Meet

1. Ensure you have granted Meet permission to access your camera and microphone. In Chrome, click the camera icon in the address bar and check that both are set to **Allow**.
2. Verify no other application is using the camera (e.g., another video call app).
3. Refresh the Meet tab and rejoin the meeting.

## Search issues

### Search results seem outdated or inaccurate

Google's index updates continuously, but some pages may take time to be re-crawled. If you are looking for the most recent news, click the **News** tab in search results or use the **Tools** option to filter by date.

### SafeSearch settings not saving

If SafeSearch keeps resetting, it may be locked by your network administrator (common on school and work networks). If you are on a personal network, make sure you are signed in to your Google Account when you change the setting at [google.com/safesearch](https://google.com/safesearch).
